FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Receiptly.

What is Receiptly?

Receiptly is a platform for creating professional receipts, managing customers and expenses, tracking payments, and understanding your business performance — all from your browser.

How do I create a receipt?

Simply enter your business and customer information, add products with quantities and prices, and Receiptly will automatically calculate the subtotal, tax, discount, and total. Then generate, download, or print your receipt.

Can I add my business logo?

Yes. You can upload your business logo and it will appear on every receipt you create.

Can I customize my receipt?

Yes. You can customize your receipt with your business name, logo, colors, footer message, currency, tax rate, and more.

Can I download or print receipts?

Yes. Every receipt can be exported as a PDF or printed directly from your browser with a single click.

Does Receiptly support multiple currencies?

Yes. Receiptly supports Naira, US Dollars, Euros, Pounds, and more. You can select your preferred currency in settings.

Can I manage customers and expenses?

Yes. You can store customer details, view their payment history, and record business expenses to see them alongside your sales.

Can I access Receiptly on mobile?

Yes. Receiptly is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

How do Receiptly subscription plans work?

Every account starts on the Free plan with unlimited receipts. The Normal plan adds customer management, expense tracking, and payment tracking; the Pro plan adds analytics and the Pro receipt template. You can request an upgrade any time from Settings → Billing, by bank transfer or card — an admin confirms the payment and activates the plan.
